Crop Guide
Quick reference for common Tamil Nadu crops

🌾 Paddy (Rice)

Best sown June–July (Kuruvai) or Aug–Sept (Samba). Needs standing water, clayey/alluvial soil. Watch for stem borer and blast disease.

Water-intensive90-150 days

🥜 Groundnut

Sow after monsoon onset, prefers sandy loam soil, moderate water. Watch for leaf spot and collar rot in wet conditions.

Drought-tolerant100-120 days

🎋 Sugarcane

Plant Dec-Jan or June-July. Needs deep, well-drained soil and heavy irrigation. Watch for red rot and early shoot borer.

High water need10-12 months

☁️ Cotton

Sow with monsoon (June), suits black soil best. Moderate water. Watch for bollworm and whitefly infestations.

Black soil150-180 days

Knowledge Base
Common questions, answered simply
How accurate is the soil photo analysis? ▾
It gives a rough visual estimate of soil type only — not lab-accurate nutrient levels. For precise fertilizer advice, share your soil health card values in Chat.
Can I use this without typing? ▾
Yes. Tap the microphone button in Chat and speak your question in Tamil or English.
What does the confidence badge mean? ▾
It shows how certain the AI is about a diagnosis. Low confidence means you should consult your local Krishi Vigyan Kendra before acting.
Is my data shared with anyone? ▾
Your profile settings are stored only on your own device.
